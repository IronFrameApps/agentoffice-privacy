Privacy Policy

Agent Office • Ironframe Apps • com.ironframe.agentoffice
Last updated: November 5, 2026

Agent Office lets you chat with Claude, an AI assistant from Anthropic, inside a 3D office. This policy explains what happens to your information.

What stays on your phone

  • Your staff list, notes, settings, daily limit and spend meter.
  • Your own Anthropic API key, if you choose to add one in Settings.
  • Voice input uses the speech recognizer built into Android. We never receive your audio.

What we collect

  • Messages. The text you send to Claude, together with your staff list, notes and purpose so Claude has context. It is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, and on to Anthropic, only to produce a reply. We do not store your messages or Claude's replies.
  • An anonymous account ID. The app creates it automatically; there is no sign-up and we never ask for your name or email. We keep it with a count of how much you used the service each day, to enforce limits and prevent abuse.
  • Subscription check. If you subscribe, the app sends your Google Play purchase token with each message. Our server uses it only to ask Google Play whether your subscription is active, and keeps a one-way hash of the token and the answer.
  • Your own API key (optional). If you add one, it is sent with each message through our server to Anthropic so the request can be made on your account. We use it only for that request and do not store or log it.

Who handles it for us

  • Anthropic generates Claude's replies and processes your messages under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Supabase hosts our server and the anonymous account.
  • Google Play handles purchases and subscriptions.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking.

Computer Office (optional)

If you buy the Computer Office unlock, your phone talks directly to the companion app on your own computer. Nothing is relayed through our servers. What Claude can see and do on that computer is limited to the folders you choose, and anything beyond reading waits for your approval on your phone.

Deleting your data

Staff, notes and settings are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. To have your anonymous account ID and usage counts deleted from our server, email us and send the request from the same phone if you can.

Children

Agent Office is not meant for anyone under 18.

AI replies

Replies are generated by AI and can be wrong. Do not rely on them for medical, legal or financial decisions.
